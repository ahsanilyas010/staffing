Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

Assorted Staffing is the data controller responsible for your personal information collected through this website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, location, CV/resume, and employment preferences when you register as a candidate or contact us.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To match your profile to suitable employment opportunities
  • To communicate with you about relevant roles
  • To share your profile with potential employers (only with your consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We will never share your personal data with any employer or third party without your explicit prior consent. We do not sell your data under any circumstances.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@assorted.group.

6. Data Retention

Active candidate profiles are retained for 12 months from the date of registration. After this period, data is deleted unless you request an extension.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@assorted.group
