Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you submit your resume, we collect your name, email address, phone number, location, desired role, and the CV/resume file you upload. We may also collect other information you voluntarily provide.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to match you with open roles within our client network. A recruiter reviews every submission personally. We do not share your details with any employer without your explicit consent.

How long we keep it

Candidate profiles are retained for 24 months from the date of submission. After this period your data is securely deleted unless you request an extension or have been placed in an active role.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at privacy@assorted.group.

Security

All uploaded files are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised recruiters only.
